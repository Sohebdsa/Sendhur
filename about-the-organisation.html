About the Organisation

Sendhur Educational Trust is dedicated to transforming lives through education and skill development, with a particular focus on paramedical sciences.

More than an organisation

Sendhur Educational Trust is dedicated to transforming lives through education and skill development, with a particular focus on paramedical sciences. Founded on the principles of social upliftment and community service, our trust is committed to providing accessible, high-quality training and support to individuals from rural and underprivileged backgrounds.

More than an organisation, it is a movement towards a brighter, healthier and more prosperous future for all. We invite you to join us in this transformative journey — together, we can achieve lasting impact and create opportunities for a better tomorrow.

Key Initiatives and Achievements

01

Paramedical Training

Since 2005, our flagship institution, Sri Meenakshi College of Paramedical Sciences, has been at the heart of our educational efforts — with modern classrooms, laboratories, a library and hostel facilities. Our programmes, supported by the Government of Tamil Nadu and the Tamil Nadu Skill Development Corporation, have empowered over 500 beneficiaries, improving their career prospects and earning potential.

02

Community Impact

We have partnered with initiatives such as Magalir Thittam and the Youth Skill Training Programme to extend our reach. These collaborations have positively impacted around 1,500 individuals, providing them with valuable skills and employment opportunities.

03

Healthcare Outreach

Recognising the health challenges faced by workers in industrial areas, especially around Dindigul, we established the Dindigul Allergy Asthma Research Foundation. It focuses on screening and addressing lung health issues prevalent among workers in spinning units. Through regular free lung screening camps and outreach programmes, we provide critical health services and raise awareness about preventive measures.

04

Post-Tsunami Support

In response to the devastation caused by the 2004 tsunami, we extended our training programmes to Nagapattinam, helping survivors rebuild their lives through skill development and employment opportunities.

Infrastructure and Facilities

Our training centre is designed to provide a supportive and effective learning environment.

  • Modern laboratories and classrooms
  • Comprehensive library and learning resources
  • Hostel accommodation for outstation students

Our facilities are approved by the Directorate of Medical Education, Chennai, and the King Institute of Preventive Medicine.
