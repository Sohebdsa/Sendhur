Sendhur Training Programme

We provide knowledge in the field of paramedical science through diploma programmes at Sri Meenakshi College of Paramedical Sciences in Dindigul, Tamil Nadu.

Our programmes combine classroom learning with practical laboratory work, supported by modern laboratories, a library, lecture halls and hostel facilities for outstation students.

For details on eligibility, documents and fees, please contact the trust office directly.
