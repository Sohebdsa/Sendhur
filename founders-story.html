About Us

Founder's Story

Founded in 2003 by Dr. M. Gopinath, Sendhur Educational Trust began with a belief in the power of education and skill-building.

A beacon of hope and empowerment

Sendhur Educational Trust is dedicated to transforming lives through education, skill development and healthcare. Established with a vision to uplift underserved and marginalised communities, our organisation is committed to creating equitable opportunities and fostering sustainable development.

Founded in 2003 by Dr. M. Gopinath, a distinguished Allergy and Asthma Therapist with extensive training and experience, Sendhur Educational Trust was born out of a deep-seated belief in the power of education and skill-building.

Dr. Gopinath's qualifications, including an M.B., B.S., DFM and CTCD, and his commitment to social service laid the foundation for an organisation that seeks to address critical gaps in education and healthcare.

Founder's Story

In 2003, Dr. Gopinath founded Sendhur Educational Trust in Dindigul to address critical gaps in education and healthcare for rural and underprivileged families. Under his leadership, the trust opened Sri Meenakshi College of Paramedical Sciences in 2005 and later established the Dindigul Allergy Asthma Research Foundation, which screens spinning-unit workers for lung disease.

The trust also extended its training programmes to survivors of the 2004 tsunami in Nagapattinam, and today works with the Government of Tamil Nadu, the Tamil Nadu Skill Development Corporation, TAHDCO and NULM to train people for employment.
