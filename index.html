Who We Are

Sendhur Educational Trust is dedicated to transforming lives through education and skill development, with a particular focus on paramedical sciences. Founded on the principles of social upliftment and community service, we provide accessible, high-quality training and support to individuals from rural and underprivileged backgrounds.
