Sri Meenakshi Montessori School

Igniting young minds. Building bright futures.

Igniting young minds.
Building bright futures.

At Sri Meenakshi Montessori School, we believe every child is unique and deserves a joyful, engaging and nurturing learning environment. Located within the serene campus of Sri Meenakshi College of Paramedical Science in Dindigul, we offer a safe and vibrant space for your child's early education journey.

Each programme follows the Montessori method, encouraging self-directed learning, hands-on activities and emotional growth.
